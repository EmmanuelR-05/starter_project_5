adapted by retaining alphabetic French tokens of at least three characters,
normalizing accents to ASCII for compatibility with the game's letter tiles,
and sorting and deduplicating the results.

The wordfreq data combines information from multiple sources. Its attribution
notices are documented at:
https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq/blob/912caf64b657478d1dff1138efdc078947d54bb1/NOTICE.md

ParaCrawl, and OPUS OpenSubtitles 2018. The wordfreq project asks that Robyn
Speer be credited by that name.
